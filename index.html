Working together

Start with one
well-defined problem.

A focused pilot gives us something concrete to evaluate before extending the work across more systems.

01

Understand the current system.

Identify the people, dependencies and constraints involved. Agree on the outcome and how we’ll verify it.

02

Build a useful first version.

Work on a bounded task, demonstrate progress and test against real requirements. Keep uncertainty visible.

03

Leave the team able to use it.

Deliver the code, checks and documentation. Agree on deployment, handover and any ongoing support.
